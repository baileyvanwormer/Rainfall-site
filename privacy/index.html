Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Rainfall: Focus & Study Timer ("Rainfall") is made by Bailey Van Wormer. This policy explains what information Rainfall uses and how it's handled.

The short version

Rainfall doesn't collect, sell, or share your personal information. Everything the app needs to work stays on your iPhone.

Information stored on your device

Rainfall saves a few things locally on your iPhone so the app works the way you expect:

  • The name you enter, used only to greet you in the app
  • Your settings, like your interval, session length, and music choice
  • Your session history, such as minutes studied and loops completed

This information isn't sent to me or to anyone else. Deleting the app removes it from your device.

Information I don't collect

Rainfall doesn't use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and it doesn't collect your location, contacts, photos, or identifiers for tracking you across apps or websites.

Sharing

If you choose to share a session summary, Rainfall opens the standard iOS share sheet. What you share, and with whom, is up to you and is handled by the app you share to.

Children

Rainfall isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and its effective date.
