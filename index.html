How it works

Choose your rhythm

50/10, 45/15, or the classic 25/5, and a session length that fits your day.

Focus with music

Study under a calm sky with the soundtrack that fits your mood.

Break in the rain

When a focus block ends, the music fades into gentle rain so you know it's time to step away.

See what you got done

When you finish, see how long you studied and how many loops you completed.
