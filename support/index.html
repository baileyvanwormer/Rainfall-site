Frequently asked questions

How does a session work?

Choose an interval (like 25/5, meaning 25 minutes of focus and 5 minutes of break), a session length, and a music style, then tap Start focus. Rainfall loops focus and break blocks until the session ends.

What happens during a break?

The music fades out and gentle rain sounds play until it's time to focus again.

I can't hear any music.

Check that your ringer volume is up and that your iPhone isn't connected to a different Bluetooth device. You can also try pausing and resuming the session.

Can I pause, skip, or end a session early?

Yes. During a session, use the controls at the bottom of the screen to pause, skip to the next block, or stop. Your summary shows what you finished.
